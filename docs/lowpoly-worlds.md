# Low Poly 3D: World Ideas

30 low-poly world scenes for the library. Each is one scene with orbit controls, a time-of-day or palette control, and (where it fits) the walk-around person from the Racing Circuit.

For each idea: **Look** (what you see), **Wow** (the thing that makes people stop), **Play** (what you can do in it).

---

## Part 1: The first 10

### 1. Pandora Grove
- **Look:** a night jungle of glowing blue and purple plants, giant trees with hanging vines, floating rock islands.
- **Wow:** touch the ground or a plant and light ripples out through the whole forest.
- **Play:** walk through; plants pulse as you pass; spore lights drift up.

### 2. Dune Sea
- **Look:** orange dunes with long wind ripples, half-buried ruins, a huge sun low on the horizon.
- **Wow:** a giant sandworm moves under the sand, raising a ridge that cracks open as it passes.
- **Play:** drag to change wind direction and watch the ripples and blowing sand follow.

### 3. Interstellar Corn Field
- **Look:** endless swaying corn rows under a huge dusty sky, a lone farmhouse, a water tower.
- **Wow:** wind rolls across the field in waves; a drone glides over and the corn bends under it.
- **Play:** walk a path through the corn; a dust storm slider turns the sky from blue to brown.

### 4. Ice Moon Outpost
- **Look:** a frozen plain with a gas giant filling the sky, a small research base, blowing snow.
- **Wow:** an aurora ripples overhead and tints the snow.
- **Play:** switch the base lights on and off; rotate the planet's day.

### 5. Neon Rain City
- **Look:** a cyberpunk street at night, wet roads, glowing signs, steam vents.
- **Wow:** rain streaks and puddles reflect every sign; flying cars cross between towers.
- **Play:** change the sign colours; rain intensity slider.

### 6. Floating Islands
- **Look:** sky islands joined by rope bridges, waterfalls pouring off the edges into cloud.
- **Wow:** the islands slowly drift and bob, and the bridges sway.
- **Play:** walk across the bridges; a wind slider.

### 7. Underwater Reef Kingdom
- **Look:** coral towers, a sunken ship, schools of fish, light rays from above.
- **Wow:** a whale shadow passes over the whole reef.
- **Play:** the camera is a diver; fish scatter as you swim close.

### 8. Lava Planet
- **Look:** black rock plateaus cut by glowing rivers, a red sun, falling ash.
- **Wow:** vents erupt in time, throwing glowing rock that lands and cools.
- **Play:** eruption rate slider; heat-haze toggle.

### 9. Overgrown Ruins
- **Look:** a lost temple taken over by jungle, mossy columns, god-rays through the canopy.
- **Wow:** fireflies gather and swirl around the central altar.
- **Play:** time of day moves the light rays across the stone.

### 10. Desert Planet Outpost
- **Look:** two moons over a salt flat, a crashed spaceship, a small nomad camp.
- **Wow:** a slow day-to-night cycle with the camp's lamps coming on.
- **Play:** walk between camp and wreck; a sandstorm toggle.

## Part 2: 20 more, stranger and bigger

### 11. The Whale Road
- **Look:** a calm sea with enormous whales swimming in the sky above a fishing village.
- **Wow:** the whales are the size of mountains; their songs shake the water and make the lanterns sway.
- **Play:** a boat you can steer; whales dive and surface as you pass.

### 12. Library at the End of the World
- **Look:** a cliff-edge reading room of endless shelves, with pages drifting out into a starry void.
- **Wow:** loose pages float up and arrange into shapes; the floor is glass over the stars.
- **Play:** open a book and its pages fan out; candle light follows you.

### 13. Tidal Moon Village
- **Look:** a village of stilt houses on a moon with a huge planet sitting low in the sky.
- **Wow:** the planet's pull makes the sea rise and fall once a minute, flooding and exposing the streets.
- **Play:** a tide slider; boats rise with the water.

### 14. Clockwork Valley
- **Look:** a valley where hills are giant gears, rivers are brass channels, and trees are springs.
- **Wow:** every gear is linked, so turning one sets the whole valley in motion.
- **Play:** drag a gear to wind the world up; time-of-day follows the main wheel.

### 15. Mushroom Cathedral
- **Look:** a cave of tower-sized mushrooms glowing in layers of teal and pink.
- **Wow:** spores drift like snow and flash when they touch the water pool.
- **Play:** knocking a cap releases a burst of light; a depth-of-cave slider.

### 16. Sky Train Highlands
- **Look:** a steam train crossing a high viaduct between snowy peaks, with clouds below the rails.
- **Wow:** the track continues out over a gap as the train lays it ahead of itself.
- **Play:** speed lever; camera on the engine, the roof or a passing hawk.

### 17. Crystal Canyon
- **Look:** red canyon walls full of huge glowing crystals that hum.
- **Wow:** light bounces between crystals and paints rainbow bands on the rock.
- **Play:** a sun-angle slider moves the rainbow bands across the walls.

### 18. The Last Lighthouse
- **Look:** a lone lighthouse on a rock in a storm, with huge waves and lightning.
- **Wow:** each lightning strike lights the whole scene for a moment, and the beam cuts through the rain.
- **Play:** storm strength slider from calm to wild; the beam sweeps with a clear rhythm.

### 19. Bonsai Planet
- **Look:** a tiny round planet with a single huge tree, small houses and a path wrapping round it.
- **Wow:** the planet turns under you while you walk, so you walk forever around a small world.
- **Play:** seasons slider turns the tree from blossom to green to red to bare.

### 20. Monsoon Market
- **Look:** a crowded night market under a sheet of rain, paper lanterns and striped awnings.
- **Wow:** hundreds of tiny low-poly people with umbrellas moving through the lanes.
- **Play:** rain on or off; a lantern colour palette.

### 21. Dragon Spine Mountains
- **Look:** a mountain range that is clearly the back of a sleeping dragon, with snow along its spine.
- **Wow:** every so often it breathes, and the whole range rises and falls, shedding snow.
- **Play:** wake it up: it lifts a wing and the clouds scatter.

### 22. Tundra of Lights
- **Look:** a snowy plain at night under a huge aurora, with a small cabin and a sled.
- **Wow:** the aurora reflects in a frozen lake and cracks the ice with colour as it moves.
- **Play:** aurora strength and colour palette; a walking figure with a lantern.

### 23. Terraform Garden
- **Look:** a small dome in a dead red desert, with one green patch inside.
- **Wow:** the green spreads outward across the sand as you watch, turning rock to grass.
- **Play:** click to plant; each tree starts the green growing from that point.

### 24. Giant's Playground
- **Look:** a normal backyard seen from an insect's size: huge toys, a garden hose river, grass like trees.
- **Wow:** the scale: a child's marble is a boulder and a dropped cookie is a building.
- **Play:** the camera is a beetle; knock over a block tower.

### 25. Orbital Garden Ring
- **Look:** the inside of a huge spinning space ring with farms, rivers and towns curving up overhead.
- **Wow:** you can see the land curve up on both sides and meet above you.
- **Play:** a spin-speed slider changes how the horizon bends; day and night from the sun-strip.

### 26. Coral Sky Archipelago
- **Look:** islands made of coral floating in an orange sunset, with manta-ray kites.
- **Wow:** rays glide between the islands in formations, trailing glowing plankton.
- **Play:** time of day; lure the rays with a lantern.

### 27. Haunted Harvest
- **Look:** an autumn farm at dusk, with scarecrows, pumpkins and a crooked barn.
- **Wow:** scarecrows turn their heads to follow the camera; the pumpkins light up one by one.
- **Play:** a spookiness slider from cosy to creepy; the moon rises.

### 28. Sunken Cathedral Lake
- **Look:** a still lake with the tops of a drowned cathedral rising out of it, mist and fireflies.
- **Wow:** the reflection shows the whole cathedral continuing deep under the water.
- **Play:** water level slider reveals or hides more of the building.

### 29. Caravan of Giants
- **Look:** a desert with huge walking creatures carrying whole villages on their backs.
- **Wow:** the villages are alive: smoke from chimneys, washing in the wind, people on the bridges between creatures.
- **Play:** speed slider; the camera rides one of the giants.

### 30. Paper Kingdom
- **Look:** a whole world made of folded paper: paper mountains, a river of blue paper, origami birds.
- **Wow:** the landscape unfolds and folds up again as you scroll, flat to 3D and back.
- **Play:** scroll drives the fold; a pen sketches a new tree onto the map and it folds up out of the page.

## Suggested build order

Best value first, since each one shows off a different strength of the library:

1. **Pandora Grove** (glow and interaction)
2. **Dune Sea** (scale and a big moving thing)
3. **Interstellar Corn Field** (wind and a walkable world)
4. **Whale Road** (the most striking single image)
5. **Clockwork Valley** (the most interactive)
6. **Paper Kingdom** (the most unusual, and a nod to the "flat to fully 3D" headline)

## Shared pieces to build once

- **Sky and time-of-day:** sun, moons, stars, aurora, gradient sky (used by almost every scene).
- **Wind field:** one wind value that bends corn, grass, flags, trees and sand.
- **Walker:** the person from the Racing Circuit, with W A S D and a follow camera.
- **Particles:** drifting spores, ash, snow, fireflies, rain (instanced, so they stay cheap).
- **Water:** a flat low-poly water plane with tide, waves and reflection.
