# Parts of a whole

[Back to all chart types](../README.md)

- [Pie / Donut Chart](pie-donut.md) — Slices of a circle sized by share of the total.
- [Treemap](treemap.md) — Nested rectangles whose area is proportional to value.
- [Sunburst Chart](sunburst.md) — Concentric rings; each ring is one level of a hierarchy.
- [Waffle Chart](waffle.md) — A grid of squares (often 10x10) with a share filled in.
- [Stacked Bar (Share) Chart](stacked-bar.md) — One bar divided into segments.
