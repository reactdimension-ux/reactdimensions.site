# Slope Chart

**Group:** Other

## What it shows
Two points per item joined by a line (before vs after).

## Best for
Change between two moments across many items.

## Avoid when
More than two time points (use a line chart).

## Data it needs
Item + value at time A and B.

## Variants
Labelled ends, highlight one item.

## Suggested props (React Dimensions component)
`data, highlight, labels, colors`

Every option should be a prop with a default, with `prefers-reduced-motion` respected and an accessible text summary (`role="img"` with an `aria-label`, or a visually hidden data table).
