# Population Pyramid

**Group:** Other

## What it shows
Two bar sets mirrored around a centre axis.

## Best for
Comparing two groups across ordered categories (age by sex).

## Avoid when
Groups do not share categories.

## Data it needs
Category + two values.

## Variants
Absolute vs percent, animated by year.

## Suggested props (React Dimensions component)
`data, leftLabel, rightLabel, normalize, colors`

Every option should be a prop with a default, with `prefers-reduced-motion` respected and an accessible text summary (`role="img"` with an `aria-label`, or a visually hidden data table).
