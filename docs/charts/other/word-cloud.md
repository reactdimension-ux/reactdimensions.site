# Word Cloud

**Group:** Other

## What it shows
Words sized by frequency.

## Best for
A quick, decorative overview of text.

## Avoid when
Anything needing accuracy.

## Data it needs
Word + weight.

## Variants
Random, spiral layout, shaped, rotating.

## Suggested props (React Dimensions component)
`words, minFont, maxFont, rotation, spiral, colors`

Every option should be a prop with a default, with `prefers-reduced-motion` respected and an accessible text summary (`role="img"` with an `aria-label`, or a visually hidden data table).
